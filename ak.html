THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

Meet APLI.

Animals and Plants Logger and Identifier

OUR MISSION

More moments in nature.
More curiosity about our world.

APLI is an educational prototype designed to encourage children aged 7–11 to spend more time outdoors, notice local biodiversity, and learn through playful discovery.

🌎
HOW IT WORKS

From camera to collection

The proposed APLI device captures photos outdoors. Later, an app helps identify plants and animals and awards playful experience points for discoveries. This website is an early version of the app experience.

THE EXPERIMENT

Can exploration change habits?

The proposed study invites 25 children aged 7–11, with parent/guardian consent, through three 3-day phases: without APLI, with APLI, and without APLI again. Parents record outdoor time and primary-device screen time; a short questionnaire provides feedback. The study journal is a local data-entry tool, not validated research software, and no study results are claimed.

⚑
Prototype limitation

Participant profiles and PINs are stored ONLY in this browser; they do not sync between devices and are not secure online accounts. Use Export/Import to back up progress before clearing browser data. Photo classification uses a general model and may be wrong. A future version needs consent-based, authenticated cloud storage and specialized local wildlife recognition.

PARTICIPANT PROFILES

Keep your discoveries

Use a nickname and 4–8 digit PIN to keep separate journals on this device. This is a local profile only—not a cloud account. Export your collection to transfer or back it up.

A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. Clearing browser storage will erase local profiles unless you exported the data. Do not reuse an important password.
